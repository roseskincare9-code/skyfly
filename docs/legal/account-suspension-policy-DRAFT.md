# Skyfly Account Suspension and Reactivation Policy

## How Skyfly can restrict access (as built)
| Action | Effect | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Business locked | The workspace shows the renewal screen; published pages and stored data are kept | Super Admin (Platform admin → business) or subscription expiry |
| Account disabled | The person is signed out everywhere, cannot sign in or receive reset emails; the business and its data are untouched | Super Admin (Platform admin → Accounts), with a recorded reason |
| Reactivation | Lock removed / account re-enabled; access returns as before | Super Admin, recorded in the audit log |

## When
- **Non-payment:** after the paid-until date the business is locked; data kept for [period] before any deletion, with [notice by email].
- **Policy breach (Acceptable Use Policy):** a warning first where reasonable; immediate suspension for fraud, security abuse, illegal products or risk to others.
- **Security:** compromised accounts may be disabled at once; the owner is contacted to recover access.
- **Legal request:** as required by law.

## Notice and appeal
We tell the account owner the reason (unless the law or security prevents it) and how to appeal at [support email]. Appeals are reviewed by a person within [N] business days. **(Counsel:** notice periods; consumer law.**)**

## Records
Every lock, unlock, disable and reactivation is kept in the audit log with who, when and why.
