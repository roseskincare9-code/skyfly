# Skyfly Acceptable Use Policy

Applies to every Skyfly account, workspace, landing page and integration.

## Not allowed
1. Illegal products or services, or products you are not licensed to sell. **(Counsel:** NAFDAC rules for cosmetics/health products.**)**
2. Misleading selling: invented reviews or testimonials, false stock or scarcity, fake countdowns, false before/after or medical claims, hidden fees.
3. Spam: marketing messages to people who did not consent; ignoring unsubscribe requests.
4. Abuse of other people's data: collecting customer data you do not need, selling it, or using it beyond what your customers expect.
5. Security abuse: trying to access another business's workspace or data, probing or attacking Skyfly, automated sign-ups, sharing accounts to bypass plan limits, uploading malware.
6. Impersonation of other businesses, brands or people; using pages for phishing.
7. Content that is hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative or that infringes others' intellectual property.

## What we may do
Remove content, pause landing pages, restrict features, or suspend accounts under the Account Suspension Policy. Report illegal activity to authorities where required.

## Reporting
Report abuse to [abuse email] with the page address and what you saw.
