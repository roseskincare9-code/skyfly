# Business Pro vs Agency VIP: proposal for the owner's decision

Status: **proposal.** Prices are decided (Business Pro ₦199,000/month, Agency VIP ₦399,000/month); the contents are not.
Skyfly now enforces per-plan limits for **landing pages** and **team members** (Platform admin → Platform settings → Plan limits).
They are blank (no plan-specific limit) until you enter them, so nothing changes for existing businesses until then.

## Proposed comparison
| | Business Pro (₦199,000/mo) | Agency VIP (₦399,000/mo) | Enforced by Skyfly today? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private business workspace | 1 | 1 per paid client workspace (see note) | Yes (one workspace per registration) |
| Landing pages | **10** (proposed) | **30** (proposed) | Yes, once you enter the numbers |
| Team members (incl. owner) | **3** (proposed) | **10** (proposed) | Yes, once you enter the numbers |
| Orders, customers, products | Unlimited (fair use) | Unlimited (fair use) | Per-business order rate limits protect the platform |
| Checkout, WhatsApp hand-off, order emails | Included | Included | — |
| Tracking (Meta, TikTok, X pixels) | Included | Included | — |
| Google Sheets copies | Included | Included | — |
| Email marketing automations | Included | Included | — |
| Custom domain for pages | 1 | Several | Not enforced yet (Super Admin sets up) |
| Support | Standard | Priority | Process, not software |

**Agency note:** an agency usually runs several client businesses. Today one registration creates one workspace;
the Super Admin can create more workspaces for the same owner. Decide whether Agency VIP includes a number of
client workspaces (e.g. 3) or is priced per client workspace. Enforcing a workspace count needs a small change
once you decide.

## To decide
1. The numbers in bold (or your own).
2. Agency VIP: client workspaces included, and how extra ones are billed.
3. Whether trials get Business Pro limits.
Then enter the limits in Platform settings; choosing a plan for a business applies its landing-page limit.
